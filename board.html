Asset board

Every 3D object in the game is generated by Tripo. A song is split into its instruments, each instrument's sound is measured, and those measurements become the prompt for its model. Every noun the singer sings becomes an object too. A different song gives a different world.

  1. Songany track
  2. StemsDemucs splits it into instruments
  3. Soundhow bright, dense and noisy each one is
  4. Promptbuilt from what was measured
  5. Tripotext to textured 3D model
  6. Worldplaced where you hear it
